ai-visualized

From-scratch, interactive visual explainers for modern AI and ML. Each one is a working implementation paired with a step-by-step picture of the mechanism.

Self-Attention, Visualized →

A 0.8M-parameter GPT trained on Shakespeare, re-implemented from scratch in the browser, with its attention drawn character by character and token-by-token generation.

Word Embeddings, Visualized →

Words become vectors: nearby means similar, and directions carry meaning. A hand-built toy, then ~250 real GloVe vectors with nearest-neighbour and analogy tools.

Training a Word Embedding, Step by Step →

Watch an embedding form. Skip-gram starts every word as a random dot and, one inspectable nudge at a time, pulls words that share context together. Step through it or press Play.

Word2Vec, on Real Text →

The same algorithm on a whole book (or your own pasted text). It pulls out the frequent words and you watch them drift into groups by the company they keep; click a word for its nearest neighbours.

Prompt Injection, Visualized →

A sandboxed, simulated support bot — try known prompt-injection attacks, turn defenses on one at a time, and see exactly which one stops each attack and why.
